Platform Instructions

Overview

IGNITE SCOUT is a catalog intelligence platform designed to identify independent artists who own their catalogs and are viable candidates for licensing, buyout, or A&R opportunities. The system cross-references multiple data sources to verify ownership and flag conflicts automatically.

How CSV Exports Work

The platform processes CSV exports from Chartmetric. These must be exported in a specific format:

  • Export Format: UTF-8 encoded CSV with headers
  • Required Columns: Artist Name, Chartmetric ID, Monthly Listeners, Career Start Year
  • File Naming: Artists Values - YYYY_MM_DD - HH_MM_SS.csv
  • Export Source: Chartmetric → Artist Lists → Export as CSV
  • Upload: Drag and drop into the Queue panel or click the + CSV button
  • Processing: Each CSV is processed sequentially. The percentage shown indicates completion progress of ownership verification across all APIs.

API Integrations

iTunes Search API

Searches the iTunes/Apple Music catalog to identify P-line holders (℗ line). Checks who owns the sound recording rights. If a third-party label appears as the P-line holder, it flags the artist for review.

Deezer API

Cross-references artist releases on Deezer to verify label/distributor information. Identifies if releases are distributed through known independent distributors (DistroKid, TuneCore, etc.) or third-party labels that indicate existing deals.

Genius API

Retrieves artist social media links, bio information, and associated accounts. Used to find contact information (Instagram, Twitter/X, Facebook, website) and verify artist identity. Social links appear in the same reading section alongside iTunes and Deezer data for each artist.

Chartmetric

Primary data source. Provides artist metadata, distributor/label information from their database, monthly listener counts, career timelines, and growth metrics.

Groq & Gemini (AI)

AI models used for intelligent classification. They analyze the combined signals from all APIs and determine whether an artist should be marked as KEEP, REVIEW, or DROP based on ownership patterns, label affiliations, and deal indicators.

Classification System

  • KEEP — All sources show artist name or known independent distributor. Safe to proceed with outreach.
  • REVIEW — Mixed signals detected. Third-party labels found on some sources, or no data returned. Requires manual verification.
  • DROP_THIRDPARTY — Third-party label confirmed across sources. Artist likely does not own their catalog.
  • DROP_MAJOR — Major label affiliation detected. Not a viable opportunity.

Reading the Feed

Each artist card in the feed displays:

  • Status Badge: KEEP / REVIEW / DROP classification
  • Career Start: Year the artist first appeared on streaming platforms
  • Chartmetric: Distributor or label info from Chartmetric's database
  • iTunes: P-line holders found across releases
  • Deezer: Label/distributor data from Deezer's catalog
  • Genius: Social links (Instagram, Twitter, website) for contact research
  • Reasoning: AI-generated explanation of the classification decision

Daily Workflow

  1. Export artist list from Chartmetric (CSV)
  2. Upload CSV to the Queue
  3. System processes artists through all APIs
  4. Review KEEP artists for outreach readiness
  5. Manually verify REVIEW artists
  6. Skip DROP artists
  7. Target: 100 artists reviewed / ~20 qualified per day

Priority Order

  1. Licensing Opportunities — Always the primary focus
  2. Buyout Opportunities — Larger catalogs with proven history
  3. A&R Opportunities — Emerging artists for signing
